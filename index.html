About Zenith

Taking the City to New Heights

Zenith Grill and Lounge crowns The St. Regis Amman on the seventeenth floor, offering a vibrant dining and nightlife experience high above the city. Indoor and outdoor venues set a lively tone against a breathtaking backdrop of glittering city lights.

Spanning 1,370 square meters, the space blends indoor and outdoor seating for every season. An upscale, stylish interior balances intimate corners with vibrant social areas, inviting you to lounge, socialize or dine.

The Name

The Highest Point in the Sky

In astronomy, the zenith is the point in the sky directly above where you stand. Atop The St. Regis Amman, it stands for elevation, excellence and a perspective unlike any other in the city.

Meet the Chefs

The Culinary Artistry

Culinary artistry at Zenith is led by Chef Naiz, Chef de Cuisine, whose passion for refined flavours and modern grill techniques shapes every dish.

Inspired by global influences and premium ingredients, Chef Naiz and his team combine refined technique with creativity, building a culinary identity that reflects Zenith's vibrant spirit. Every dish tells a story of craftsmanship and collaboration.
